Privacy Policy

EFFECTIVE 04 NOV 2026 · VERSION 1.0 · WRITTEN FOR HUMANS FIRST

The short version: Liquid Core has no server. There is no account, no login, no analytics, no telemetry, no ad SDK, and no crash reporter. The AI model runs entirely on your device's silicon. Your conversations, notes, tasks, alarms, voice recordings, and learned preferences never leave the hardware you are holding. When this app is offline — which is most of the time by design — it still works, because there was never anything on the other end of a wire to begin with.

"If it doesn't leave your device, it can't be subpoenaed, breached, sold, or trained on by someone else."

Network access — the complete list

Liquid Core touches the network in exactly three situations, all of which happen only when you explicitly trigger them or during first-time setup:

  • First download of the model & runtime. The Qwen3 0.6B ONNX weights are fetched once from Hugging Face (via jsDelivr CDN), plus the JavaScript libraries (transformers.js, TensorFlow.js, Three.js) and fonts. After this one-time fetch, everything is cached by the service worker and the app runs with the radio switched off.
  • Optional updates. If you press "re-fetch weights" or the app shell updates, the same CDNs are contacted. You will see progress on-screen while it happens.
  • Nothing else. Ever. No pings, no heartbeats, no usage reporting, no "phone home" on launch. Open your firewall logs: after setup, Liquid Core generates zero traffic.

On-device learning — how your data makes the model yours

While you use the core, each exchange is recorded locally as a training example. When enough examples accumulate (or you press TRAIN NOW), TensorFlow.js runs real gradient steps on a small low-rank adapter attached to the model's output path. The result: the assistant drifts toward your phrasing, your names for things, and your routines. That adapter — the distilled memory of how you talk — is stored on your device in IndexedDB and applied at inference time. It is never uploaded, because there is nowhere to upload it to. Pausing learning stops capture instantly; "FORGET EVERYTHING" deletes every example, adapter, note, task, alarm, and preference in one action, with no confirmation dark-patterns.

Children

Liquid Core collects nothing from anyone, including children. It is not directed at an audience that would require parental consent flows, precisely because there is no collection, no accounts, and no transmission to report.

Your controls

  • Pause learning — LEARN.CYCLE card → PAUSE LEARNING.
  • Forget everything — LEARN.CYCLE card → FORGET EVERYTHING (wipes IndexedDB + localStorage).
  • Purge all — OFFLINE card → PURGE ALL (also clears the service-worker caches, including model weights).
  • Disarm the ear — one tap; microphone stream is stopped and tracks are released immediately.
  • Export — your notes and tasks are plain rows in IndexedDB; the app offers a JSON dump of anything it stored, because it was always yours.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the version number and date above change and the app shows a one-time notice on next launch. We do not email you, because we do not have your email. We do not push a silent update, because the app is open-source and auditable at github.com/Kavyant420/p.

Contact

Questions about this policy belong in the repository issue tracker. There is no legal inbox, no data protection officer, and no request form — because there is no data to request. Everything this app knows, you can read, export, or delete on your own device, today, in under ten seconds.
